# YorkerAPI season pack

The last completed season of cricket as CSV, free to use under the [YorkerAPI Sample Data Licence](../DATA-LICENSE.md). Use it in anything you build, credit YorkerAPI, and don't republish the files themselves.

**Download:** the latest `yorkerapi-season-pack.zip` from this repository's [season-pack release](https://github.com/sports-data-hq/yorkerapi-mcp/releases/tag/season-pack). `preview/` holds the first 100 rows of a few files.

Seasons: 2025, 2025-26, 2026. Season naming: the year a season or series starts. Competitions, by their API id: bbl, blast, blast-women, bpl, county, cpl, ecb-women-s-one-day-cup, european-t20-premier-league, hundred, hundred-women, ilt20, ipl, lpl, major-clubs-limited-over-tournament, major-clubs-t20-tournament, major-league-tournament, mlc, odi-men, odi-women, one-day-cup, plunket-shield, psl, sa20, super-smash, super-smash-women, t20i-men, t20i-women, test-men, wpl. No odds, props or play-by-play: those, every earlier season and live data are on the API, free to start at https://yorkerapi.com/signup?utm_source=github&utm_medium=pack&utm_campaign=yorkerapi-pack.

## Columns

- **batting**: `league`, `season`, `player`, `innings`, `not_outs`, `runs`, `balls_faced`, `high_score`, `fours`, `sixes`, `fifties`, `hundreds`, `average`, `strike_rate`
- **bowling**: `league`, `season`, `player`, `matches`, `balls`, `overs`, `runs_conceded`, `wickets`, `best_match_wickets`, `economy`, `average`, `strike_rate`
- **elo**: `league`, `team`, `format`, `elo_end_of_season`, `as_of`
- **innings**: `league`, `date`, `team1`, `team2`, `innings`, `batting_team`, `bowling_team`, `super_over`, `runs`, `wickets`, `overs`, `declared`, `forfeited`, `target_runs`
- **matches**: `league`, `season`, `date`, `format`, `gender`, `event_name`, `event_stage`, `event_match_number`, `team1`, `team2`, `venue`, `city`, `toss_winner`, `toss_decision`, `winner`, `result`, `win_by_runs`, `win_by_wickets`, `win_by_innings`, `method`, `player_of_match`
- **standings**: `league`, `season`, `team`, `played`, `wins`, `losses`, `ties`, `no_result_or_draw`, `points_2_1_1`
